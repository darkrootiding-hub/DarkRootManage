About DarkRoot

Our Team

DarkRoot is a collaborative team platform focused on task management, structured workflow, and professional growth. Our primary goal is to build a strong, disciplined, and high-performance team environment.

Development & Skill-Building Phase

All members are required to complete tasks in the Task Section. Performance will be strictly evaluated based on task completion, consistency, and discipline.

Starting from March 19, DarkRoot uses a performance-based system where 1 DarkRoot Point = 0.5 NPR. Points are earned only after successful completion of assigned tasks.

Salary distribution will be implemented after one month based on total points earned, performance consistency, and overall contribution.

No task completion = No points = No salary.
